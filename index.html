Project overview

This project evaluates whether assigning customer orders to alternative factories could reduce estimated transportation distance and cost.

Business question: Could different factory assignments improve shipping efficiency while lowering estimated transportation costs?

Methodology

1. Prepare data

Organize customer, factory, order, and location data.

2. Compare distances

Compare customer-to-factory distances and potential alternative assignments.

3. Estimate costs

Use the project's assumption of $1 per kilometer to estimate transportation costs.

4. Recommend

Summarize potential savings and reassignment opportunities.

Results and assumptions

The model estimates current transportation cost of $20.41 million and optimized cost of $8.31 million, implying potential savings of about $12.10 million (59.28%) under project assumptions.

Important: These are estimates, not confirmed realized savings. The model assumes $1/km and depends on data quality. Validate route distances, freight rates, factory capacity, service levels, and operational constraints before implementation.
